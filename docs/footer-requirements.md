# DLG Satellite Site — Footer Requirements (v1)

A reusable footer/disclosure spec for DLG satellite websites (calculators,
tools, informational microsites). Copy this into each new project.

> ⚠️ Attorney-advertising and consumer-privacy rules vary by state (Texas,
> California, etc.). This spec is a practical baseline — the final wording and
> which items apply MUST be reviewed and approved by DLG's advertising-
> compliance counsel before launch.

---

## STEP 0 — Classify the site (pick the modes that apply)

| Mode | When it applies | Adds requirements from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Informational tool** | Always — every satellite site | §1 |
| **B. Names/links a law firm** | The site mentions or links a specific firm (e.g. DLG) → that content is **attorney advertising** | §1 + §2 |
| **C. Collects personal info** | The site has a form/intake that collects name, phone, email, etc. | §1 (+ §2 if it also advertises) + §3 |

Most DLG tool sites are **A**, or **A + B** (a soft "talk to DLG" block).

---

## STEP 1 — ALWAYS required (every site, Mode A)

**Visible on-page in the footer (conspicuous — not hidden behind a link):**
- Not legal advice
- Results are approximate estimates (if the site has any calculator/estimate)
- Using the site creates no attorney–client relationship
- Recommendation to consult a licensed attorney

**Links (a policy document behind a link is fine):**
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Disclaimer
- About (optional)

---

## STEP 2 — ADD when the site names or links a law firm (Mode B = attorney advertising)

**Visible on-page — placed BOTH at the advertising content (the block that names
the firm) AND noted in the footer:**
- The label **"Attorney Advertising"**
- **Responsible attorney/firm identification**: attorney name, firm, city/state
  (add State Bar number + principal office if the state requires it)
- **Prior results disclaimer**: prior results do not guarantee a similar
  outcome; recoveries vary
- **No obligation**: no obligation to hire any attorney
- **"No fee" clarification** (only if the site says "no fee / no win no fee"):
  refers to attorney's fees only; the client may still owe case costs/expenses
- **Slogan/dramatization note** (only if used): dollar figures are illustrative,
  not a promise; images/scenarios may be dramatizations; "best"/"top" are
  slogans, not claims of superiority

---

## STEP 3 — ADD when the site collects personal info / routes leads (Mode C)

**Visible directly AT the form, next to the submit button (not in the footer,
not behind a link):**
- **TCPA consent** (whenever a phone number is collected)

**Behind a link is fine:**
- **"Do Not Sell or Share My Info"** — CCPA/CPRA opt-out mechanism (only if you
  sell or share data). Provide a working opt-out (form or email) and honor
  Global Privacy Control (GPC).
- Privacy Policy must state **what is collected and who it is shared with**.

---

## RULE OF THUMB (placement)

> Anything needed to keep the ad/content from being **misleading** must be
> **conspicuous on the page**. Anything that is a **policy document** or an
> **opt-out mechanism** may live **behind a link**.

## COPY-READY TEXT (English — paste into the site)

Replace `[Site]`, `[State]`, `[Attorney]`, `[Firm]`, `[City, State]`, `[Bar No.]`.

### A. Universal footer disclaimer (Mode A)
> **Not legal advice.** [Site] provides free self-help tools and general legal
> information for [State] personal-injury claims. It does not provide legal
> advice or legal services. Results are approximate estimates and may differ
> from your actual situation. Using this site does not create an attorney–client
> relationship. For advice about your specific situation, consult a licensed
> [State] attorney.

### B1. Footer add-on when the site advertises a firm (Mode B)
> This site contains attorney advertising.

### B2. Responsible-attorney line, shown at the advertising block (Mode B)
> **Attorney advertising.** [Attorney], [Firm], [City, State][, State Bar No.
> [Bar No.]] is responsible for this advertising. Contacting the firm does not
> create an attorney–client relationship.

### B3. Fuller advertising disclosures (Mode B — include the lines that apply)
> Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome, and recoveries vary. You are
> under no obligation to hire any attorney. "No fee unless you win" refers to
> attorney's fees only; you may still be responsible for case costs and
> expenses. Any dollar amounts shown are illustrative estimates, not a promise
> of recovery; any images or scenarios may be dramatizations, and terms such as
> "best" are marketing slogans, not a claim of superior quality.

### C1. TCPA consent — next to a form's submit button (Mode C)
> By clicking "Submit," you agree that [Firm] may contact you at the phone
> number and email you provide — including by autodialed or pre-recorded calls
> or texts — about your potential claim, even if your number is on a Do-Not-Call
> list. Consent is not a condition of any service, and message and data rates
> may apply. You may opt out at any time.

### C2. Do Not Sell / Share (Mode C — linked page)
> [Site] does not sell your personal information. If you are a California
> resident (or a resident of a state with similar rights), you may still request
> that we not sell or share your personal information under the CCPA/CPRA. To
> opt out, email [privacy@domain] or use the form below. We honor Global Privacy
> Control (GPC) where required by law.
